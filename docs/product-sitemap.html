OOutline Monitor
Product map

Everything the platform does, page by page

Outline Monitor watches a client's websites around the clock: every page, every enquiry form, every SSL certificate and every domain or hosting expiry date. It sends one alert when something breaks and one when it recovers, and it measures website traffic with a one-line tracking script. Each client works in its own workspace; the platform team runs everything from a separate Super Admin console.

How it works

The mechanisms behind the screens, in the words a client hears them explained with.

Website & page monitoring

The homepage and every discovered page are requested from the server at the client's plan interval. A failure is re-tried before it counts, so a single hiccup never raises an alert.

  • Detects timeouts, DNS errors, refused connections, HTTP 4xx / 5xx, redirect loops, SSL errors, missing expected text and parking pages
  • Pages come from the sitemap, the WordPress sitemap and internal links, up to the plan's page limit
  • Several pages failing in one scan produce one combined alert

Form monitoring

Each form is filled in and submitted the way a visitor would, either over HTTP or in a real headless browser for popup and JavaScript forms, and the response is judged.

  • Outcomes: working · submission failed · server error · timeout · needs attention (configuration)
  • reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha and Turnstile forms are reported as CAPTCHA protected, never faked and never marked broken
  • Email delivery of the test enquiry can be verified with an IMAP test mailbox

Alerts state machine

One alert when a problem starts, silence while it continues, one alert when it is resolved. Applies to websites, pages, forms, SSL, domains, hosting and the platform's own mail server.

Working→Failed→Alert sent→Still failing · silent→Recovered→Recovery sent
  • Recipients: workspace admins, the assigned staff member and, when opted in, the client contact
  • Every alert stays in the Alert History with downtime, notification count and the error

SSL, domains & hosting

Certificates are checked with a full TLS handshake: validity, hostname match, chain, expiry date and issuer, with warnings before expiry.

  • Domain and hosting reminders are based on the expiry dates recorded per record, at 30 and 10 days and on expiry; a renewed date closes the reminder

Analytics & the tracking script

A single line, <script src="…/analytics/SITE-XXXXXXXX.js">, placed before </head>. The portal shows "Tracking active" only after the first real page view arrives.

  • Visitors, unique visitors, sessions, page views, page-level visitors, bounce rate, time on page, live visitors
  • Referrers, UTM campaigns, devices, browsers, operating systems, countries, regions and cities (approximate, IP-based)
  • No cookies, IP addresses stored only as salted hashes, data kept for the plan's retention period

Plan-based execution, no cron

Every website, form and certificate carries its own next-check time computed from the client's plan. The application keeps its own scheduler alive: a background worker it starts itself, a request chain inside the web server, and a watchdog on every incoming request.

  • A 60-minute plan is checked every 60 minutes; a 5-minute plan every 5 minutes
  • Changing a plan re-aligns the schedule immediately; expired subscriptions stop, renewals resume
  • Nobody has to stay logged in and no browser tab has to be open

What it does not do

Domains and hosting are expiry reminders on dates you record; there is no WHOIS or DNS lookup. Location is approximate and IP-based; the tracking script never asks the browser for precise location. Analytics reports engaged time from the pages a visitor leaves, not wall-clock session length, and counts "returning" visitors as visitor-days minus new devices. Form email verification needs an IMAP test mailbox; without one the test result reads "email delivery not verified".
